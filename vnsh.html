Vnsh

2023

VNSH is a lighting work in which light escapes through a slit in a 5 mm-diameter aluminum pipe and illuminates the wall. The slender pipe seems to disappear into the glow it creates.

We associate light with clarity and safety, yet a bright reflection on a river can conceal its depth. This contradiction led us to question how readily we equate illumination with understanding. By reducing the fixture to a barely perceptible line, VNSH draws attention to the experience of light before the object producing it.

Material
Aluminum, stainless steel, LED
Dimensions
II: W250 D250 H1500 mm
III: W300 D240 H1000 mm
Award
Designart Tokyo Under 30, 2023
