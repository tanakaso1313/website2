Design Excellence Award 2024

December 10, 2024

We are honored to announce that the Liminal Lamp series has been recognized with the prestigious Japan Design Excellence Award for 2024. This recognition celebrates innovative lighting design and exceptional craftsmanship that bridges traditional Japanese aesthetics with contemporary functionality.

The Japan Design Excellence Award, established by the Japan Institute of Design Promotion, recognizes outstanding design achievements that contribute to the enhancement of quality of life through superior design. The Liminal Lamp was selected from over 1,200 submissions across multiple categories.

The award jury praised the Liminal Lamp for its "masterful integration of traditional Japanese joinery techniques with contemporary lighting technology, creating an object that exists beautifully between sculpture and function." The judges particularly noted the lamp's ability to transform spaces through subtle gradations of light and shadow.

"This recognition validates our approach to design as a practice of careful observation and thoughtful making," reflects So Tanaka. "The Liminal Lamp emerged from studying the quality of light in traditional Japanese interiors and asking how we might capture that ephemeral beauty in a contemporary object."

The lamp's distinctive form is achieved through a complex process of steam-bending solid wood, requiring collaboration with master craftspeople who possess decades of experience in this specialized technique. Each lamp requires approximately 40 hours of handwork, from initial wood preparation to final assembly.

The award ceremony took place at the Tokyo International Forum, where the Liminal Lamp was displayed alongside other winning designs. The recognition includes a touring exhibition that will travel to design museums in Osaka, Kyoto, and Kanazawa throughout 2025.

Award Details:
Award: Japan Design Excellence Award 2024
Category: Lighting Design
Recognition: Innovation in Traditional Craft Integration
Ceremony: December 10, 2024, Tokyo International Forum
