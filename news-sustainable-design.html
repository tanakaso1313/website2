Sustainable Design Initiative

January 20, 2025

We are proud to announce the launch of our new sustainable design initiative, a comprehensive approach to creating furniture that honors both environmental responsibility and the rich traditions of Japanese craftsmanship. This initiative represents a fundamental shift in how we source materials, work with artisans, and consider the lifecycle of our designs.

The initiative focuses on three core principles: locally sourced materials from certified sustainable forests, collaboration with traditional craftspeople to preserve ancient techniques, and designs that prioritize longevity and repairability over disposability.

Working exclusively with timber from forests within 200 kilometers of Tokyo, we ensure that transportation impacts are minimized while supporting local forestry communities. Each piece of wood is selected not just for its aesthetic qualities, but for its story—trees that have naturally fallen or been selectively harvested as part of responsible forest management.

Our partnership with master craftspeople represents a commitment to preserving traditional joinery techniques that eliminate the need for metal fasteners or synthetic adhesives. These methods, refined over centuries, create stronger bonds than modern alternatives while maintaining the natural character of the wood.

"Sustainability isn't just about materials—it's about creating objects that people will treasure for generations," explains So Tanaka. "When we combine thoughtful design with traditional craftsmanship, we create pieces that improve with age rather than deteriorate."

The first collection under this initiative, launching Spring 2025, will feature five new pieces that demonstrate these principles in practice. Each design will include detailed information about its material origins, the craftspeople involved, and care instructions to ensure decades of use.
