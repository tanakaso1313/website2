Let a Colored Paper Swim in the Clouds

2025

Developed for a solo exhibition at Moro, Let a Colored Paper Swim in the Clouds continues the experiments begun in Transfer.

Colored paper is selected and a mold prepared, but the imagined form is only a beginning. Once white cement is poured, the materials undergo a transformation that remains hidden until the mold is opened. Color changes in unexpected ways, and the final shape cannot be fully controlled. Opening the mold brings the pleasure of discovering something that could not have been planned.

The paper is no longer there, yet its presence can still be felt. Its color has entered the cement, carrying something of their time together into the finished object.

Material
White cement, colored paper
