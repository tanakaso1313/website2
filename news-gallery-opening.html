New Gallery Partnership

February 28, 2025

Exhibition Installation Views

Opening Reception

We are excited to announce a new partnership with Axis Gallery Roppongi for exclusive showings of limited edition pieces and experimental designs. This collaboration represents a significant milestone in bringing contemporary Japanese craft to a wider international audience.

Axis Gallery, located in the heart of Tokyo's design district, has been a champion of innovative Japanese design for over two decades. Their commitment to supporting emerging designers aligns perfectly with our studio's mission to explore the boundaries between traditional craft and contemporary form.

The partnership will begin with a solo exhibition featuring the complete Vnsh collection, including several pieces never before shown publicly. The exhibition will run for six weeks and include an artist talk and workshop series exploring the philosophy behind liminal design.

"Working with Axis Gallery allows us to present our work in a context that celebrates both innovation and craftsmanship," says So Tanaka. "Their space provides the perfect environment for visitors to experience the subtle qualities that define our approach to design."

Exhibition Details:
Opening: March 5, 2025, 6:00-8:00 PM
Run: March 5 - April 16, 2025
Location: Axis Gallery, 5-17-1 Roppongi, Minato-ku, Tokyo
Artist Talk: March 12, 2025, 7:00 PM
