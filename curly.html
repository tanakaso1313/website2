Curly

2026

Curly is a table lamp that draws its form from material memory, utilizing the curvature paper retains from being rolled and wrapped during storage and transport. Anchored by minimal hardware to a cylindrical aluminum base, the curling sheet creates a self-supporting shade that diffuses a warm, soft light, while a coiled wire carries that same curved gesture outward.

Material
Japanese washi paper, aluminum
