ALL FURNITURE LIGHT OTHER SHOP BIO CONTACT

My work draws from personal reflection and engagement with materials, philosophy, and interdisciplinary artistic experiments.

I view the selection of materials, form, and manufacturing processes as inherently linguistic, each choice functions as a vocabulary through which I both interpret the world and communicate my perspective to others.

My practice is motivated by a desire to reimagine and transform relationships with others, with society, with the environment, and with my evolving self. Understanding that our world is fundamentally constituted through such connections, I approach each project as an opportunity to explore these essential bonds and create new possibilities for how we might relate to one another and to the world we inhabit.
