MO Lamp


2025

MO Lamp begins with a single raw block of montmorillonite. It is a clay mineral most of us touch without realizing, present in the soil, in household goods, in skincare, but rarely seen as itself. I wanted to leave the stone as it is, and let it stand as a product.

Compared with most minerals, montmorillonite is built from extraordinarily thin, plate-like particles stacked in many layers. When the stone fractures, its broken surface reveals a nano-scale texture, a fine, restless geography that only fully opens under light. The lamp is a way of drawing that out, with slow shadows moving across a quiet surface.

The irregular form of the stone is paired with a geometric counterpart: an aluminum disc that catches the LED's soft glow and casts it onto the wall and back across the rock. Light radiates outward and draws attention to the way the stone fractures, exposing layers built up over millions of years.


Material: Montmorillonite, aluminum, LED

Size: variable
